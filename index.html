Equity Research Desk

Full-stack · Quant

Type a ticker, get an analyst-style research profile in 8–15 seconds: a plain-English business summary, forward catalysts sorted into confirmed / developing / risk, and a five-factor quantitative score — all built on free data sources and free-tier APIs.

  • Factors come from the academic literature rather than invention: EBIT/EV (Greenblatt), 12-1 momentum (Jegadeesh & Titman), gross profitability (Novy-Marx), Sloan accruals, and idiosyncratic volatility (Ang et al.).
  • Every factor is scored as a sector percentile rank against the S&P 500, from a locally built 503-name factor snapshot — the same way institutional factor shops score stocks. Weights shift by sector, because gross profit means nothing for a bank.
  • An earnings-quality gate: when reported income runs far ahead of cash generated, the badge flips to FLAGGED and the composite caps at 40.
  • Engineering: one merged LLM call per report, automatic model fallback on quota exhaustion, a fully parallel data pipeline, and graceful degradation so any single source can fail without blocking the report.

MLB Hybrid Season Simulator

Simulation · ML

A season-projection engine that fuses team-level Elo ratings with bottom-up player projections through a logistic classifier, then drives the result through a 10,000-trial vectorized Monte Carlo of the schedule and postseason bracket. The forecasting problem here is structurally the same one as in markets: separating signal from variance over a long horizon.

  • Validated, not just built. A walk-forward backtest rebuilds the pipeline exactly as it would have existed on Opening Day 2024 and 2025 — no future data — and scores it blind. The 80% win bands cover 81.7% of actual team-seasons, and playoff-odds Brier (0.206) beats the climatology baseline (0.240).
  • I found and fixed my own leak. Training on same-season roster stats leaked September performance into April predictions. Switching to prior-season features dropped in-sample accuracy from an inflated 0.572 to an honest 0.550 — the honest number is the one shipped.
  • Two uncertainty sources are modelled separately: per-trial latent talent draws (σ calibrated by backtest sweep) and per-game Bernoulli noise. Scenario comparisons share random seeds, so deltas are causal rather than noise.
  • Ablations are trained and reported alongside the main model — hybrid beats Elo-only beats baseline in out-of-sample log-loss, which is the evidence that the roster feature earns its place.

Summit Therapeutics — Post-WCLC Review

Equity research

A research note on Summit Therapeutics (Nasdaq: SMMT), a single-asset biotech whose entire value rests on one bispecific antibody. Written after WCLC 2026 delivered the first Phase III overall-survival win against Keytruda — and argues that the result, while real, does not answer the question the stock is actually priced on.

  • The work is in separating what the data proved from what it didn't. HARMONi-2 is a China-only, interim, secondary-endpoint, monotherapy result. The two subgroups that matter most for Summit's own pivotal trial — PD-L1 1–49% and non-squamous — both still cross 1.
  • Neutral, with the thresholds stated in advance: positive on a squamous final PFS HR ≤0.70 that holds in Western patients; negative on a miss or an Asia-only benefit. Saying what would change your mind before the readout is the part that's testable afterwards.
  • Every number is tied to one of 31 numbered sources — company filings, the 10-Q going-concern language, trial registries, and the conference presentations themselves.

Aging Genes in Arthropods — Structured Literature Review

Research · Capstone

My HKU capstone: a structured review of 41 papers asking whether stress-response genes — above all heat shock proteins — actually drive aging in arthropods. The headline result is a negative one about the field itself. The literature is large and active, but it almost never runs the test its own framing implies.

  • Built as a six-sheet queryable database rather than a reading list: one row per paper across 16 coded fields, a 0/1 flag matrix so multi-valued tags count correctly, live-formula summaries, and a stress-type × hallmark evidence map that makes the empty cells visible.
  • The finding is the gap. HSP70 carries 51% of the literature and loss of proteostasis 71% — but only 29% of papers measure lifespan or age-related change at all, and just two manipulate a gene and then show a longevity effect. Telomere attrition and cellular senescence have zero papers.
  • Every cleaning decision is logged in a Data_QA sheet — species synonyms that had been double-counted, malformed gene entries, and three papers whose hallmark labels were non-standard and left flagged as Unmapped rather than quietly forced into a category.
  • It also records what the data cannot support: the source sheet carries no publication years, so trends over time are not analysed rather than estimated. Coded throughout against the López-Otín hallmarks of aging framework.
